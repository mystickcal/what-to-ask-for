Content moderation

Also called: moderation API, abuse reporting, user generated content safety, NSFW filter, trust and safety

Checking what users post or generate for abuse, and having a way for people to report it and for you to act. Combine an automatic classifier, user reports, a review queue and clear actions like hide, warn and ban.

You need it when

  • Users can post content others see, or your AI generates content from user prompts.

Skip it when

  • Private, single-user content nobody else can see. Your AI provider's own policies still apply.

Ways to build it

  1. A moderation API $0 for OpenAI

    OpenAI's moderation endpoint (omni-moderation-latest) is free to use and checks text and images against categories like harassment, hate, self-harm, sexual content and violence.

    Tradeoff: Quick to add. Thresholds need tuning, and it doesn't know your community's rules.

  2. A self-hosted classifier $0 + GPU or CPU

    Open-weight safety models like Llama Guard run on your own hardware or a hosted inference provider.

    Tradeoff: Data stays with you and categories can be customized. You run and update the model.

  3. Reports and a review queue $0

    A Report button on every post, a queue in admin with the content and reporter, and actions that are logged.

    Tradeoff: Catches what classifiers miss. Someone has to work the queue.

Prompt for your coding agent

Replace the [bracketed] parts. Works in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.

Add content moderation to [feature: posts / comments / AI generations].

Requirements:
- Check new content (and AI prompts and outputs) with [OpenAI moderation / chosen classifier] before it's shown to others. Block clear violations, hold borderline ones for review, and publish the rest.
- Make thresholds per category configurable, and log scores without storing more content than needed.
- Add a Report button with a reason picker. Reports go to an admin review queue.
- Admin actions: hide, restore, warn user, suspend user. Every action is written to the audit log.
- Rate-limit posting and reporting.
- Write a short content policy page and link it from the post form.

Acceptance checks (run each and show me the result):
1. A known harassment test string is blocked or held.
2. A normal post publishes with no delay users notice.
3. Reporting a post puts it in the admin queue with the reason.
4. Hiding a post removes it for everyone else, and the action appears in the audit log.

If you skip it

Abuse that drives users away, harmful AI output under your brand, and app store or payment provider trouble.

People describe this as

  • “users can post anything on my site”
  • “stop people generating harmful content with my AI feature”
  • “add a report button and a review queue”
  • “filter abusive messages between users”
