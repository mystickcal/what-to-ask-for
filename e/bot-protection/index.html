Bot and spam protection

Also called: CAPTCHA, Turnstile, honeypot field, signup spam, fake accounts, credential stuffing

Stopping automated traffic on forms that cost you money or reputation: signup, login, contact, checkout and anything that sends email or calls an AI. Layers work best: a hidden honeypot field, a challenge like Turnstile checked on the server, and rate limits behind both.

You need it when

  • You see bursts of fake signups, spam messages, or failed card attempts.
  • A form triggers something that costs money, like an email, SMS or AI call.

Skip it when

  • Low-traffic internal tools behind a login.

Ways to build it

  1. Honeypot field and timing $0

    A field hidden from people that bots fill in, plus a check that the form wasn't submitted in under a second or two.

    Tradeoff: Invisible to users. Only stops simple bots.

  2. Turnstile, hCaptcha or reCAPTCHA free tiers

    Add the widget, then verify the token on your server with the provider's siteverify API before doing anything. Cloudflare Turnstile tokens are single-use and expire after 300 seconds.

    Tradeoff: Stops most automated abuse. A widget that isn't verified on the server does nothing.

  3. Rate limits and verification $0

    Limit by IP and by account, require email verification before the account can do costly things, and use your payment provider's fraud tools on checkout.

    Tradeoff: Catches what gets past the challenge. Needs tuning so real users aren't blocked.

Prompt for your coding agent

Replace the [bracketed] parts. Works in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.

Add bot protection to [signup / contact / checkout] forms.

Requirements:
- Add a honeypot field hidden with CSS (not type=hidden), with tabindex=-1 and autocomplete=off so people and password managers skip it. Reject submissions that fill it, with a generic message.
- Add Cloudflare Turnstile (or hCaptcha). On the server, verify every token with the siteverify endpoint before any other work. Reject missing, failed, expired or reused tokens.
- Keep the secret key on the server only.
- Rate-limit each form by IP and by email or account.
- New accounts must verify their email before they can [send invites / use AI / start a trial].
- Log rejected attempts with the reason (no personal data) so I can see the volume.

Acceptance checks (run each and show me the result):
1. Submitting the form with curl and no token is rejected.
2. Reusing a token that already passed is rejected.
3. Filling the honeypot field is rejected.
4. A real user in a normal browser signs up with no extra steps beyond the widget.
5. The siteverify secret never appears in the page source.

If you skip it

Fake accounts, a spam-filled inbox, email sending suspended for bounces, card-testing fees and disputes, and AI bills run up by scripts.

People describe this as

  • “bots are signing up thousands of fake accounts”
  • “my contact form gets spam”
  • “add a captcha to signup”
  • “someone is testing stolen card numbers on my checkout”
