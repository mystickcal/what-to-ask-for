Free trial (with or without a card)

Also called: trial period, no card required trial, card-required trial, trial_period_days, trial abuse

A period of paid features before the first charge. Asking for a card up front means fewer people start, but more of them end up paying. Without a card more people try it, and you must decide what happens at the end: cancel, pause, or ask for a card.

You need it when

  • People need to use the product before they will pay for it.

Skip it when

  • A free plan already lets people try the core feature.

Ways to build it

  1. Stripe trial with a card collected Stripe fees

    Checkout in subscription mode with subscription_data.trial_period_days. The first charge happens when the trial ends.

    Tradeoff: More of the people who start end up paying. Fewer start, and you need a reminder before the first charge.

  2. Stripe trial with no card Stripe fees

    Add payment_method_collection=if_required and set trial_settings.end_behavior.missing_payment_method to cancel or pause, so a trial without a card ends cleanly.

    Tradeoff: More starts. You need in-app prompts to collect a card before the end.

  3. A trial clock in your own database $0

    Store trial_ends_at on the account and grant paid features until then. Only send people to Checkout when they choose to pay.

    Tradeoff: No payment provider involved until they pay. You build the reminders, the expiry and the abuse checks yourself.

Prompt for your coding agent

Replace the [bracketed] parts. Works in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.

Add a [7]-day free trial [with / without] a card.

Requirements:
- One trial per account. Record trial_used_at so canceling and signing up again on the same account doesn't start a new one.
- [Stripe] Use Checkout with subscription_data.trial_period_days=[7][, payment_method_collection=if_required and trial_settings.end_behavior.missing_payment_method=[cancel/pause]].
- During the trial, show the days left on every page and a button to add a card.
- Handle customer.subscription.trial_will_end (sent 3 days before the end by default) and send our own reminder email with the end date and what happens next.
- When the trial ends without payment, remove paid features and keep the user's data.

Acceptance checks (run each and show me the result):
1. A new account gets paid features for exactly the trial length.
2. The same account can't start a second trial after canceling.
3. With a Stripe test clock, the trial_will_end webhook sends one reminder email.
4. A no-card trial that ends without a card loses paid features and keeps its data.
5. A card-required trial makes the first charge on the end date.

If you skip it

Surprise charges and refund requests, endless repeat trials, or trials that never end.

How Derek runs it

Custody Journal has a notifications command that sends its own trial-ending emails 3 days and 1 day before the trial ends. Each reminder goes out once per subscription, and it is skipped for users who turned that email off or are in their quiet hours.
