Refunds and chargebacks

Also called: disputes, chargeback handling, refund flow, credit notes, payment reversals

The flow for giving money back, and for answering when a customer's bank pulls it back for you. Your app listens for refund and dispute events, updates access, and keeps the records you need to respond to a dispute before the deadline.

You need it when

  • You take payments. Refund requests and some disputes come with any volume.

Skip it when

  • You use a merchant of record that handles refunds and disputes for you. Still listen for their events.

Ways to build it

  1. Refund from your payment dashboard $0, original fees usually not returned

    Issue refunds by hand in the Stripe, Paddle or Lemon Squeezy dashboard and let webhooks update your app.

    Tradeoff: Fine at low volume. Make sure your app reacts to the webhook, not to your memory.

  2. A refund action in your admin $0

    An admin button that calls the refund API with a reason and amount, logs who did it, and adjusts access.

    Tradeoff: Consistent and auditable. Needs role checks.

  3. Self-serve refunds within a window $0

    Users can cancel and get a refund within [14] days from the billing page.

    Tradeoff: Fewer support emails and fewer disputes. Some abuse risk.

Prompt for your coding agent

Replace the [bracketed] parts. Works in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.

Handle refunds and chargebacks properly in my app with [Stripe].

Requirements:
- Handle these webhooks idempotently: charge.refunded, charge.dispute.created, charge.dispute.closed (or the equivalents for my provider).
- On full refund: end paid access [now / at period end] and record it. On partial refund: record only.
- On dispute created: flag the account, email me with the deadline, and pause any automatic renewals for it.
- Admin refund action: amount, reason, logged with who did it. Only billing admins can use it.
- Keep what a dispute response needs: receipt, terms accepted at signup with timestamp, login and usage history, and support emails.
- Show refunds on the customer's billing history.

Acceptance checks (run each and show me the result):
1. Replaying a refund webhook twice changes access once.
2. A test dispute sends the alert with the response deadline.
3. The admin refund button is blocked for non-admins.
4. The evidence export for one customer can be produced in a minute.
5. Refunded charges appear in the customer's billing history.

If you skip it

Refunded users keep paid access, or disputes are lost by default because nobody saw them before the deadline.

People describe this as

  • “a customer wants their money back”
  • “I got a chargeback from the bank”
  • “remove access after a refund”
  • “issue a partial refund”
