Team invite flow

Also called: invite teammates, invitation links, workspace invites, join a team, invite by email

A way for a member to add someone by email. You create an invite with a random token, role and expiry, email a link, and on accept you check the signed-in person matches the invited address before adding them.

You need it when

  • Accounts are shared by more than one person: teams, families, clients and advisors.

Skip it when

  • Every account is single-user.

Ways to build it

  1. An invites table $0

    Rows with inviter, email, workspace, role, token hash, status (pending, accepted, declined, revoked), expires_at.

    Tradeoff: Full control over roles and edge cases. You write the emails and screens.

  2. Hosted organizations free tiers, then plan-based

    Clerk Organizations, WorkOS, Auth0 Organizations and Supabase-based kits ship invites and roles.

    Tradeoff: Fast. Your team model has to fit theirs.

  3. Shareable join links $0

    A link anyone with it can use to join, optionally limited to an email domain.

    Tradeoff: Easy for big teams. A forwarded link lets the wrong people in.

Prompt for your coding agent

Replace the [bracketed] parts. Works in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.

Add an invite flow so members can add people to a workspace.

Requirements:
- Invite form: email and role. Only roles at or below the inviter's are allowed.
- Store invite: inviter, workspace, normalized email, role, random token (stored as a hash), status (pending, accepted, declined, revoked) and expiry of [14 days].
- Email a link. If the person has no account, the link leads to signup and returns to the invite after.
- On accept, require the signed-in account's verified email to match the invited email (case-insensitive). Otherwise show who it was for.
- Accepting twice, after expiry, or after revoke shows a clear message and changes nothing.
- Admins see pending invites and can resend (new token) or revoke.
- Don't reveal whether an email already has an account. Rate limit invites per workspace.
- Log invite sent, accepted and revoked in the audit log.

Acceptance checks (run each and show me the result):
1. A user signed in as a different email can't accept someone else's invite.
2. An expired or revoked link does nothing.
3. A member can't invite someone as admin.
4. A brand-new user can sign up from the link and land in the workspace.
5. Resending invalidates the old link.

If you skip it

A forwarded invite email gives a stranger access to a private workspace, or old links work forever.

How Derek runs it

Custody Journal's co-parent invites use a random token with a 14-day expiry and pending, accepted, declined and revoked states, and set calendar and exchange-log permissions per invite. Accepting requires the signed-in account's email to match the invited email.
