Per-seat team billing

Also called: seat-based pricing, per user pricing, team plans, seat count billing, license seats

A team subscription whose quantity equals the number of paid members. Adding or removing a member changes the quantity, the payment provider prorates the difference, and your app blocks invites past the paid count if you want hard limits.

You need it when

  • You sell to teams and price grows with the number of people using it.

Skip it when

  • Plans are flat per workspace, or most customers are solo users.

Ways to build it

  1. Subscription quantity at your payment provider payment fees only

    Stripe, Paddle and Lemon Squeezy subscriptions have a quantity. Update it when membership changes and let the provider prorate.

    Tradeoff: Standard and well documented. You decide when to sync, and whether removals take effect now or at renewal.

  2. Buy seats up front payment fees only

    Admins pick a seat count. Invites beyond it ask them to buy more first.

    Tradeoff: Predictable bills, no surprise charges. One more step before inviting.

  3. True-up later payment fees only

    Let teams add people freely and bill the extra seats on the next invoice.

    Tradeoff: No friction. Some customers dislike surprise increases.

Prompt for your coding agent

Replace the [bracketed] parts. Works in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.

Add per-seat billing for team workspaces using [Stripe] subscriptions.

Requirements:
- Each workspace has one subscription. Quantity = active paid members (pending invites [do / don't] count).
- When a member is added or removed, update the subscription quantity with proration [now / at renewal]. Do it in a background job with retries.
- Store seats_paid on the workspace from webhook events, not from the API response alone.
- Only workspace owners and billing admins can change seats or see invoices.
- Show the current seat count, price per seat and next invoice estimate on the billing page.
- If seats_paid < members (failed sync or payment), block new invites and show a fix-it message. Never lock out existing members.
- Write tests for add, remove, downgrade at renewal and failed payment.

Acceptance checks (run each and show me the result):
1. Adding a member raises the subscription quantity by one within a minute.
2. Removing a member lowers it as configured, with the proration line on the next invoice.
3. A non-admin can't open the billing page.
4. A failed quantity update retries and alerts, and the app shows the mismatch.
5. Seat count in your database matches the provider after replaying webhooks.

If you skip it

Teams use 40 seats while paying for 5, or get charged for people they removed months ago.

People describe this as

  • “charge teams per member”
  • “update the bill when someone joins the team”
  • “how many seats does this workspace pay for”
  • “prorate when a team adds a user mid month”
